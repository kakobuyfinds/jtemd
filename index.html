The quick answer

A MyCNBox spreadsheet is a product research worksheet: a place to keep listing links, selected variants, measurements, prices, and review notes together. A useful entry records what you checked and when you checked it. Inclusion in a list does not verify a seller or establish product authenticity.

04 / MyCNBox spreadsheet guide

Six ways to research a find.

Thirty practical research topics, organized around the questions that make a shortlist easier to use.

01 — LINKS

MyCNBox product links

  • MyCNBox spreadsheet product links: keep the original listing URL with each item.
  • Product link organization: group entries by category and shopping purpose.
  • Broken product links: flag unavailable pages rather than guessing a replacement.
  • Seller listing comparison: confirm whether two pages describe the same item.
  • Product availability check: record the variant and the date you reviewed it.
02 — SHOES

MyCNBox spreadsheet for shoes

  • Sneaker size chart comparison: use the chart attached to the specific listing.
  • Foot length measurement guide: measure both feet and keep the units clear.
  • Shoe width and fit notes: record any width information supplied by the seller.
  • Sneaker material details: separate stated materials from appearance-based guesses.
  • Shoe colorway comparison: check the selected variant against its associated images.
03 — FIT

MyCNBox size guide

  • Hoodie chest measurement: check whether the figure is flat width or circumference.
  • T-shirt length comparison: compare with a garment you already like wearing.
  • Jacket shoulder measurement: note how the seller defines the measuring points.
  • Pants waist and inseam: keep both measurements alongside the size label.
  • Matching set size selection: verify top and bottom dimensions separately.
04 — PHOTOS

MyCNBox finds: photo checks

  • Product photo review checklist: inspect the front, back, sides, and close-ups.
  • Clothing stitching inspection: note visible seams without assuming hidden construction.
  • Fabric detail comparison: ask for composition when the listing is unclear.
  • Accessory hardware photos: inspect closures, edges, and attachment points.
  • Listing photo limitations: remember that lighting can change perceived color.
05 — COSTS

Plan a clearer shopping budget

  • International shopping budget: separate product cost from delivery and other charges.
  • Shipping quote comparison: compare quotes using the same package assumptions.
  • Currency conversion worksheet: record the rate and conversion date you use.
  • Service fee tracking: add only fees that apply to your chosen purchase route.
  • Import tax budget notes: check destination-specific rules before estimating costs.
06 — ACCESSORIES

Keep the smaller details

  • Headwear circumference guide: compare the stated adjustment range with your measurement.
  • Bracelet size measurement: record wrist size and the item's actual dimensions.
  • Jewelry material checklist: request the stated base metal and finish.
  • Underwear sizing checklist: check measurements and the seller's return conditions.
  • Jersey fit comparison: use chest, length, and sleeve measurements together.

06 / Clear answers

Before you follow a link.

What this guide does, how to use its categories, and which details still need your own checks.

What is a MyCNBox spreadsheet?

A MyCNBox spreadsheet is a product research worksheet used to organize listing links, variants, measurements, prices, and review notes. The phrase does not mean a list is official, verified, or connected to a particular service.

Where do the category links on this page go?

The category links lead to the corresponding CAFANS category pages. Other browsing links lead to the CAFANS homepage. This page does not contain a hosted spreadsheet download.

Does a spreadsheet prove that a product is authentic?

No. A spreadsheet, brand name, listing image, or photo review does not independently prove authenticity. Check provenance, seller information, and applicable purchase protections before making a decision.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

Record the listing's actual measurements and units. Compare clothing measurements with a similar garment you own, and compare foot measurements with the specific shoe listing's size chart. Record whether clothing dimensions are flat or circumferential.

Can I calculate the final delivered price here?

The calculator adds the four amounts you enter in one currency. It does not retrieve prices, calculate currency conversion, or determine tax and customs charges. Use current quotes and add any applicable costs before treating the result as a budget.

How often should product links be checked?

Reopen a listing when you update your shortlist and again before ordering. Check that the item, variant, price, stock status, and seller terms still match your notes, then record the date you checked.
